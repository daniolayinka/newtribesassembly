A people shaped for God’s purpose

New Tribes Assembly is a kingdom community committed to the Present Truth purpose of God. We believe that God continues to speak to His people, and that His Word should shape our character, relationships, decisions, and shared life. Our desire is to become all that He has called us to be and to express His kingdom in the world around us.

For us, church life is a continuing journey of formation. We seek to grow in Christlikeness, mature in our understanding of truth, and respond faithfully to what God is saying. Worship, teaching, prayer, and practical responsibility belong together in that journey.

Discovering our identity

A defining part of that journey has been discovering who we are ordained to be as a community in Christ. Our history describes this as the “Power of Divine Naming,” expressed in our identity as Gadites Community.

We understand identity as something to embrace and live out through a process of transformation. External circumstances do not determine who we are called to become. We continue to allow God’s Word to challenge, refine, and establish us as a people.

Our vision — raised believers who become light

Our vision is rooted in Isaiah 49:6, with its call to raise, restore, and become a light to the nations. We seek to raise well-rounded believers who are balanced, well-adjusted, and thoroughly established in the Present Truth purpose of God.

We describe this as raising “Raised Believers” who become light in their world and spheres of influence. Spiritual maturity should find expression in everyday life, in the way we relate, serve, work, and carry responsibility.

We also understand our calling as active participation in the life of the wider body of Christ, contributing to correction, recalibration, and reconciliation within our territory.

Local life, a global consciousness

Congress-WBN is our wider world. This connection informs how we understand our place in God’s purpose and how we serve. We live and work in a local context with the consciousness that we belong to a global community.

We understand the kingdom of God as His impact on every dimension of human life. It begins within us and finds expression through the way we live. The condition of our hearts and the values we carry give meaning to our activities and programmes.

The convictions we build with

Our foundations include pursuing and exalting the Lord in all things, remaining willing to advance as He leads, and giving ourselves to the building of mature lives. We value the development of the whole community, alongside the gifts and contributions of its members.

Community life, servanthood, and diligent work are central to this commitment. We believe human labour must be joined to divine initiative, and we seek excellence in the responsibilities entrusted to us.

Integrity, accountability, humility, and truthfulness shape the way we relate. We commit to taking responsibility for our mistakes, addressing offences directly, listening carefully, and allowing God’s will to govern our actions.

A Word we live and build with

We call the messages we receive from God “Downloads.” The name expresses our conviction that spiritual life is sustained by the Word that proceeds from Him. We return to that Word, study it together, and seek its practical application in our lives.

Our gatherings connect worship, understanding, response, and prayer. We celebrate God’s presence and our oneness as a community, engage with His Word, and pray into the matters it brings before us. Our response to what we hear is part of the continuing conversation between God and His people.

Growing together

Our creed expresses a commitment to Christlikeness, sonship, and the process of becoming all God intends. We seek to remain constant and consistent through that process, strengthening one another along the way.

We believe we are created for community, fashioned for fellowship, and formed for a family. We need one another to fulfil purpose in God, and we share a responsibility to protect the integrity and values of our church family.
